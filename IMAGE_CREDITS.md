To add a colour, drop a photo into `public/images/models/` and add it to `CAR_PHOTOS` in
`src/app/data/car-photos.ts`.

To swap a home-page photo, replace the file with one of the same name, or change the entry in
`PHOTOS` in `src/app/data/landing.ts`.
